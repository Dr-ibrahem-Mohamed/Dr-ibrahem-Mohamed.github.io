Back to the quarry

What's inside a bag of cement?

Fifty kilos of grey powder that has to hit its strength, setting time and fineness — every single time. Keeping that promise starts long before the cement mill.

Open the bag and you'll find clinker — the heart of the cement — plus gypsum to control setting time and additives chosen for each cement type.

Now rewind the kiln. Clinker is raw meal burnt at about 1450 °C — limestone, clay, sand and iron ore. These are the materials I sample, analyse and proportion every shift.
